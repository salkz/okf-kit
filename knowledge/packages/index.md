# Packages

* [cmd/okf](cmd.md) - The okf command: picks the subcommand, knows the version, and joins the two checks.
* [okf](okf.md) - Reads concept documents, records verifications and change requests, and checks a bundle.
* [review](review.md) - The review site: read a concept next to its sources, verify it or request a change.
* [kit](kit.md) - Sets a repository up with the baseline, updates it, holds it to okf.lock and checks for a newer release.
